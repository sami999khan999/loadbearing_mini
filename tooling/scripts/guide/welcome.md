This guide explains everything in the kit, one feature at a time, from scratch. It is written for
you, the **super admin**: the person who runs the whole platform, not one organization inside it.

> [!NOTE]
> Only platform admins can read this space. It is a **granted** space with no grants, so nobody
> outside the platform team can open it, and it never appears in public search or `/llms.txt`.

## How each page is laid out

Every feature page follows the same shape, so you always know where to look:

- **What it is**: the feature in one paragraph.
- **Why it exists**: the problem it solves.
- **How it works**: what happens inside, in plain words.
- **Step by step**: what to click, from a blank start.
- **Who can use it**: the permission it needs.
- **Good to know**: the traps, and the details worth knowing.

## In the order you meet it

The guide follows the life of the platform, from the first sign-in to the day a customer leaves.
Read it top to bottom the first time; after that, jump straight to what you need.

1. **Start here**: the big picture and the words you will meet.
2. **Day one: your account**: signing up, becoming the super admin, signing in and keeping the account safe.
3. **Your team**: the platform organization, inviting your staff and giving each one a role.
4. **Setting up the product**: plans, module switches and feature flags, before the first customer.
5. **Writing docs**: the guides and help pages your staff and customers will read.
6. **When customers arrive**: finding an account, and changing what one organization may use.
7. **What everyone uses**: the dashboard, notifications, document search and API keys.
8. **Keeping it running**: health checks.
9. **When a customer leaves**: exporting their data, then deleting it.
10. **Behind the scenes**: the worker, mail, where data lives, and what to do when something is red.

## Finding your way

- **Search** with **Ctrl K** (or **⌘ K** on a Mac). It searches every page of this guide.
- **The sidebar** groups the pages into sections. Click a section's name to fold it.
- **At the foot of every page** are links to the previous and next page, so you can read the guide
  straight through.
