## What it is

**Settings** → **Account**: your own name, password, email address, and the list of devices you are
signed in on. Everyone has this page; it needs no permission.

## Changing your name

Under **Profile**, edit **Your name** and press **Save changes**. You see "Saved."

## Changing your password

:::steps
### Fill in the form

Under **Change password**: **Current password**, then **New password** twice.

### Press Change password

You see "Your password is changed. Other devices have been signed out."
:::

## Changing your email address

:::steps
### Ask for the change

Under **Change email address**, type the **New email address** and press **Change email address**.

### Approve it from the old address

An email goes to your **current** address, subject **Confirm the change to your email address**.
Press **Approve the change**. Only then does the new address take over.
:::

> [!NOTE]
> The approval goes to the **old** address on purpose: someone who stole your session cannot move
> your account to their own email.

## Signing out other devices

**Where you are signed in** lists every session. Yours says **This device**. Press **Sign out** on
one, or **Sign out on all devices** for every other one. A device signed out this way can keep
working for up to a minute.
