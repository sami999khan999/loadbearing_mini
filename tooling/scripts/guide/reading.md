## What it is

Everything around a page that helps a reader find their way: the sidebar, search, the trail above
the title, the outline on the right and the links to the next page.

## The sidebar

- **Sections fold.** Click a section's name to close it, and again to open it. A section you close
  stays closed while the browser tab is open.
- **Pages inside pages** show a small arrow. The branch that holds the page you are reading opens
  by itself.
- **The space switcher** at the top, labelled **Documentation space**, moves between spaces you may
  read. It is hidden when there is only one.
- **On a phone** the sidebar becomes a drawer: **Open navigation** opens it, and
  **Close navigation** closes it.

## Search

:::steps
### Open it

Click **Search** in the sidebar, or press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac).

### Type at least two letters

Partial words match too, and small typos in titles are forgiven.

### Pick a result

Results are grouped:

| Group | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Pages** | pages in this space whose title matches |
| **In the text** | matches inside the text of this space |
| **In** *another space* | matches from each other space you may read |

Press **Enter**, or click, to open it. A match inside the text jumps straight to its heading.
:::

> [!NOTE]
> Search never shows a page you may not read. A doc hidden by an
> [access link](/docs/admin-guide/access-links) or an [audience](/docs/admin-guide/audiences) is left out.

## Around the article

- **The trail** above the title shows the sections and pages above this one.
- **On this page** on the right lists the page's headings. The one you are reading is marked as
  you scroll. Pages with no headings have no outline.
- **Previous** and **Next** at the foot follow the order of the sidebar, so the whole space can be
  read straight through.
- **Last published** at the foot shows when the page was last made live.
