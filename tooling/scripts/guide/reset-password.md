## What it is

A way back into an account when the password is forgotten, by a link sent to the account's email.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Ask for a link

On **Sign in**, press **Forgot your password?**. Type the **Email** and press **Send a reset link**.

### Open the email

Its subject is **Reset your password**. Press **Choose a new password**. The link works for one
hour.

### Choose a new password

Fill in **New password** twice (12 characters or more) and press **Save this password**. You are
taken to **Sign in**.
:::

## Good to know

> [!NOTE]
> The page always says "If that email has an account, a reset link is on its way.", even for an
> address with no account. That way nobody can use the form to find out who has an account.

- A reset **signs the account out everywhere**, so a thief who had the old password is thrown out too.
- A used or old link shows "That reset link has expired or has already been used."
- Three requests a minute are allowed from one place.
