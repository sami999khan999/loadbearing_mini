## What it is

The first screen after signing in, at `/dashboard`.

## What is on it

- **The organization's name** as the heading, and "You are … here." with your role.
- **Sections**: links to each area you have permission to use. Areas you cannot use are not shown.
- **Members**: how many members the organization has, if you may see members.
- **The bell** in the header, with your unread notifications.
- **The Organization menu** in the header, to switch organizations.

> [!TIP]
> If someone says a section is missing from their dashboard, they lack the permission for it.
> See [When something is red](/docs/admin-guide/troubleshooting).
