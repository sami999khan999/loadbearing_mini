## What it is

A **module** is one whole area of the product, such as Docs or API keys. A **module switch**
turns that area off for **every organization at once**, whatever plan they are on.

## Why it exists

For incidents. If a feature is causing harm, such as a bug that leaks data or a flood of emails,
you can make it disappear everywhere in one click while it is fixed.

## How it works

Turning a module off hides its menu entry, its pages and its actions for everyone. **Nothing is
deleted**: roles, plans and data stay exactly as they were. Turning it back on restores everything.

> [!CAUTION]
> There is **no confirmation step**, and the change applies to every organization immediately.
> The core of the app and the platform pages cannot be switched off, so you can never lock
> yourself out this way.

## Step by step: switching a module off

:::steps
### Open Platform

Scroll to **Module switches**.

### Write a reason

Each row has a **Reason** box. The **Switch off** button stays disabled until you type one, so the
next person knows why it is off.

### Press Switch off

The row now shows the reason and a **Switch back on** button.
:::

## Who can use it

`platform.module.manage`.

:::accordion{title="How this ranks against plans"}
A switched-off module beats everything: every plan, and every extra you have added for an
organization. See [An organization's entitlements](/docs/admin-guide/entitlements) for the full order.
:::
