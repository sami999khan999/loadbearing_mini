## What it is

Signing in with a Google account instead of a password.

## When it appears

The **Continue with Google** button only shows when the server has Google sign-in set up (the
settings `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`). Without them there is no button, and
nothing else changes.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Press Continue with Google

On **Sign in** or **Sign up**.

### Pick an account

Google always asks which account to use.

### You are in

If the Google address matches an existing account, the two are joined automatically.
:::

## Linked accounts

**Settings** → **Security** → **Linked accounts** lists every way into the account, such as
`credential` (email and password) and `google`. **Unlink** removes one. The last remaining way
cannot be unlinked: "This is the only way you can sign in, so it cannot be unlinked." Use
**Link a Google account** to add Google to an existing account.
